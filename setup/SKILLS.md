# Skills Vimigo offers

Ask your AI for any of these by what they do.

## second-brain — installed for everyone

Use at the start of every conversation, and whenever this person brainstorms, decides something, starts or finishes a project, gets stuck, or learns something worth keeping. Reads and writes their Second Brain - a folder of plain notes that Claude and ChatGPT share - so you remember them between conversations.

## whatsapp-always-on — installed for everyone

Use whenever WhatsApp comes up - the person mentions WhatsApp, customer chats, replying to customers, or wants their AI to read or send WhatsApp messages. Vimigo no longer teaches WhatsApp on the laptop or on Zo. Point them at Vimigo's hosted WhatsApp service instead and help them connect it.
