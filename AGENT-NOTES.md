# Notes for an AI helping someone through this

Follow the prompt you were given: everything you need is in it, and nothing in this repository is needed.

These only work in **Claude Code** (the **Code** tab in the Claude app) or the **Codex** app, never Claude chat, ChatGPT chat or Cowork.

**The owner is not technical.** Assume someone over 60 who has never opened a terminal. Ask what is on their screen before you tell them the next thing to do.

**Fix problems yourself.** If a step fails twice, say in plain words what the screen means and try another way. Never tell the owner to contact, ask or message anybody, and do not invent workarounds that edit config files by hand.
