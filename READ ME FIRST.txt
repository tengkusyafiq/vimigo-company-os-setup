VIMIGO AI SETUP
===============

This sets up your computer to work with your Zo, Claude and ChatGPT.

You do not need to know anything technical. It will tell you what to do,
one thing at a time, and it never asks you to type commands.


BEFORE YOU START
----------------

  1. Put this whole folder somewhere you can find it, like your Desktop.
     Do not run it from inside the zip file - it will not work properly.

  2. Have your phone nearby. You will need it once, to link WhatsApp.

  3. If you are on your phone's hotspot, that is fine. It will tell you
     when something is a big download.


ON WINDOWS
----------

  Double-click:  Setup-Windows.bat

  Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That is because this came
  from the internet, not because anything is wrong. Click "More info",
  then "Run anyway".


ON A MAC
--------

  RIGHT-CLICK on Setup-Mac.command, then choose Open.
  Then click Open again when your Mac asks.

  Do not double-click it the first time. Your Mac blocks anything
  downloaded from the internet until you tell it once that this is fine.
  After that first time, double-clicking works normally.


WHAT HAPPENS
------------

It checks what you already have, then does the rest for you. Anything
already installed is left exactly as it is.

Along the way it will ask you to:

  - paste your Zo account key
  - type a short code into WhatsApp on your phone
  - click Allow on a few Google permission screens
  - type your password once or twice, so your computer can install things

That is all. Everything else it does itself.

You can close it at any time and run it again later. It picks up exactly
where it left off, and it never does the same thing twice.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

Run it again. Most things fix themselves on a second run, because it
checks everything from scratch each time rather than remembering.

If it happens twice, contact Vimigo support and tell them what the
screen said.
