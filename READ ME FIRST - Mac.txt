VIMIGO AI SETUP - FOR MAC
=========================

This sets up your computer to work with your Zo, Claude and ChatGPT.

You do not need to know anything technical.


BEFORE YOU START
----------------

  Copy this WHOLE folder somewhere you can find it, like your Desktop.
  Do not run it from inside the zip file.


HOW TO START
------------

  Your Mac blocks anything downloaded from the internet until you allow
  it once. That is normal and it happens to every program. It takes four
  clicks, one time only.

  STEP 1  Double-click Setup-Mac.command.

          Your Mac will refuse, and say it "could not verify" the file.
          That is the block. It is meant to happen.

          Click DONE.

          *** Do NOT click "Move to Trash". ***
          That is the blue button, and it deletes the setup.

  STEP 2  Open System Settings.
          (the grey gear icon, or the Apple menu at the top left)

  STEP 3  Click Privacy & Security in the left-hand list, then scroll
          down to the part called Security.

          You will see a line about Setup-Mac.command being blocked,
          with a button next to it.

          Click OPEN ANYWAY.

  STEP 4  Your Mac asks for your fingerprint or your password. Give it.
          Then click Open.

  The setup now starts, and a black window appears. That window is
  meant to be there. Just follow what it says.

  After this one time, double-clicking works normally.


  If Step 3 shows no line about Setup-Mac.command, you have not done
  Step 1 yet. Your Mac only offers the button after it has blocked it
  once.


WHAT HAPPENS
------------

It checks what you already have, then does the rest for you. Anything
already installed is left exactly as it is.

Along the way it will ask you to:

  - paste your Zo account key
  - click Allow on a few Google permission screens
  - type your Mac password, so it can install things

That is all. Everything else it does itself.

One download is large: the tools for code and spreadsheets are about a
gigabyte. It will tell you before it starts, and you can do that part
later on better wifi.

You can close it at any time and run it again later. It picks up exactly
where it left off, and it never does the same thing twice.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

Run it again. Most things fix themselves on a second run.

If it happens twice, contact Vimigo support and tell them what the
screen said.
