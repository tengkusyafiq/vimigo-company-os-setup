VIMIGO AI SETUP - FOR MAC
=========================

This sets up your computer to work with your Zo, Claude and ChatGPT.

You do not need to know anything technical.


BEFORE YOU START
----------------

  Copy this WHOLE folder somewhere you can find it, like your Desktop.
  Do not run it from inside the zip file.


HOW TO START
------------

  Double-click Setup-Mac.command.

  If it opens, you are away. A black window appears - that window is
  meant to be there. Just follow what it says.


IF YOUR MAC REFUSES TO OPEN IT
------------------------------

  You will see: "Apple could not verify Setup-Mac.command is free of
  malware", with two buttons.

  *** Click DONE. Do NOT click "Move to Trash". ***
  Move to Trash is the blue one, and it deletes the setup.

  That block is your Mac being careful about anything that arrived
  through a browser. There is an easier way past it than hunting
  through settings, and it is two steps:

  STEP 1  Hold down Command and press Space.
          Type   Terminal   and press Enter.

  STEP 2  Copy the whole line below, paste it into the window that
          opened, and press Enter.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tengkusyafiq/vimigo-company-os-setup/main/install-mac.sh | bash

  That fetches a fresh copy, puts it on your Desktop, and starts it.
  Afterwards double-clicking works normally - the block does not come
  back.


  If you would rather not use Terminal, the long way round is:

    System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> scroll down to Security
    -> find the line about Setup-Mac.command -> click Open Anyway

  That button only appears after your Mac has already blocked it once,
  so do the double-click first.


WHAT HAPPENS
------------

It checks what you already have, then does the rest for you. Anything
already installed is left exactly as it is.

Along the way it will ask you to:

  - paste your Zo account key
  - click Allow on a few Google permission screens
  - type your Mac password, so it can install things

That is all. Everything else it does itself.

One download is large: the tools for code and spreadsheets are about a
gigabyte. It will tell you before it starts, and you can do that part
later on better wifi.

You can close it at any time and run it again later. It picks up exactly
where it left off, and it never does the same thing twice.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

Run it again. Most things fix themselves on a second run.

If it happens twice, contact Vimigo support and tell them what the
screen said.
